Emergency Recovery Protocol

1. Keep Emergency Mode Active

When someone scans your pet's collar tag, their smartphone captures approximate location coordinates and pings you immediately via push notification and email.

2. Safe Finder Communication

Finders can send secure messages without seeing your home address. If you've enabled direct calls in your Privacy Shield, they can call your verified number instantly.

3. Local Clinic & Shelter Alerts

Our background workers automatically flag your pet's microchip number in the regional veterinary registry so any clinic scanning the chip detects the missing report.
