Architectural Excellence

Built for Reliability, Speed & Uncompromising Privacy

Explore how PetIdentity bridges physical smart collar tags with high-performance cloud architecture to keep companions protected everywhere.

🏷️

Cryptographically Random QR Tokens

Collar tags do not contain sequential or guessable database IDs. Each tag embeds a high-entropy 128-bit cryptographic token that can be instantly revoked or replaced without deleting the pet profile.

🛡️

Zero-Leak Privacy Shield

Your phone number and physical address remain strictly masked by default. Finders contact you through an in-app secure bridge, ensuring pet security without sacrificing personal privacy.

⚡

Transactional Outbox Pattern

Database mutations and notification events commit atomically inside PostgreSQL, ensuring zero lost alerts even during high-traffic spikes.

📡

Real-time Geolocation Logging

When a finder scans the tag, their mobile browser requests approximate coordinates to immediately pinpoint the reunion point on your map.

💉

Veterinary Health & Vaccine Ledger

Integrated WSAVA vaccination catalog, manufacturer batch verification, and booster countdowns that notify you before immunizations lapse.

📱

Universal Mobile Deep Linking

Tags open the native Flutter application on iOS & Android for owners, or the ultra-fast responsive web profile for third-party finders with zero download friction.
